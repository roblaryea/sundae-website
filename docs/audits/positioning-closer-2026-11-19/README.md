## Copy decisions

- Retain the accepted **See every layer. Act in time.** homepage headline. The existing opening already covers daily operations, sales, costs, demand, intelligence, action and results. A compact bridge now explains the layers as sales, costs, staffing and daily work, the shared facts behind each team's view and how intelligence guides action.
- Keep source-dependent refresh timing explicit. Do not promise every source is real time. Confirm source coverage during setup.
- Keep the broad hospitality and food-service category. Restaurant remains appropriate for specific workflow/search pages and the R365 evaluation guide.
- Keep Core and Crew as the two offering families, with payroll inside Crew. Preserve package and paid Watchtower-extension boundaries.
- Keep the three existing recovery limits. Add an attribution principle below them rather than another dense card: a better outcome alone is not proof of cause.
- Why Sundae leads with an operating prompt, three buying reasons, authentic Core and Crew views and a walkthrough invitation. This is a scan-in-a-minute structure, not a promise that every word and product screenshot can be read in one minute.
- Solutions replaces automatically rotating screenshots and a long claim-heavy role catalogue with the two connected offerings, an authentic logbook view, six role routes and four operating-format routes. The overview is linked in desktop and mobile Solutions navigation.

Visual QA rejected the existing scheduling screenshot because it showed an empty outlet-selection state and old identity. The final Solutions proof uses the already approved native logbook capture, with populated handover records and demo-data disclosure. Both overview pages use a static, server-visible closing CTA rather than opacity-hidden scroll content.
