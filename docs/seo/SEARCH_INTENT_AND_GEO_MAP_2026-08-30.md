## Content rules

1. Lead with the operator's question, not a feature name.
2. Answer the question directly in the first useful paragraph.
3. Distinguish macro pressure from controllable operational loss.
4. Never imply that Sundae can remove wages, taxes, rates, inflation, or commodity volatility.
5. Tie product value to evidence, an accountable owner, an action, and a measured result.
6. Link every supporting article to one canonical product or solution page.
7. Use one canonical page per intent cluster to avoid internal competition.
8. Treat Robert's LinkedIn as the reach engine and the website as the durable evidence layer.

## Evidence and citation policy

- Use primary sources where possible and link to the source.
- State the scope, geography, population, period, and limits of every statistic.
- Separate reported industry evidence from Sundae product claims.
- Do not turn sector evidence into a company-specific conclusion.
- Do not publish estimated savings as achieved outcomes.
- Use measured customer results only after approval and with the measurement method stated.
