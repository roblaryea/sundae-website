# Portfolio hierarchy and buyer-journey review — 16 November 2026

## Finding and acceptance standard

The previous Home band incorrectly displayed payroll as a third peer beside Core and Crew. The product overview also used internal “substrate” language, a Pulse illustration on Crew, and four-locale feature logic that fell back to Spanish. Technical checks had not established correct hierarchy or complete editorial acceptance. These findings supersede any broad claim that the preceding release made every portfolio surface consistent.

Sundae has **two peer offerings**:

- **Sundae Core:** performance and decision intelligence. Pulse, Benchmarks, Watchtower, Insights, Ask Sundae, Foresight and measured action remain visible capabilities with their actual commercial scope.
- **Sundae Crew:** workforce and daily operations. Scheduling, attendance, people/HR, payroll, tasks, logbook and tips are subordinate capabilities. Payroll is not a third platform family; approved native calculation and country/provider boundaries remain explicit on the detailed Crew journey.

This explanation does not change the catalogue. Foresight retains its separate commercial path and included/optional treatment; package inclusions remain governed by the active catalogue and scoped estimate.

- Shared role/reason/context copy and Crew descriptors cover all 22 locales. AI-readable definitions explicitly preserve the two-offering hierarchy and package boundaries.
- JSON-LD now gives Sundae exactly two peer offering entities, Core and Crew, with Core capability relationships and payroll calculation described within Crew. This is product organization, not a new package entitlement. A semantic regression check validates the generated graph.
- Removed unsupported numerical performance badges on the touched product overview (including quantified answer latency and forecast accuracy); the claims bank requires telemetry before publishing a latency number. Existing illustrative module views now have adjacent localized disclosures. Shared offering CTAs retain their established consent-aware tracking labels, and walkthrough invitations have stable labels.
